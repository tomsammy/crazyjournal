# WE ARE STILL RELEASING BARABBAS AND CRUCIFYING JESUS.

*What if we never stopped making that choice? On moral cowardice, protecting wrongdoing for convenience, and washing our hands of complicity.*

**The Crazy Journal | November 2026**

> The crowd had a choice: Jesus or Barabbas. They demanded Barabbas—and the innocent man was handed over to be crucified. What if we never stopped making that choice? We punish honest employees because they threaten powerful interests, protect wrongdoing because the people responsible are useful to us, sacrifice competence for connections, and then wash our hands and pretend we had nothing to do with the outcome.

![We Are Still Releasing Barabbas and Crucifying Jesus](/images/journals/releasing-barabbas.jpg)

The crowd had a choice.

Jesus or Barabbas.

They demanded Barabbas.

And the innocent man was handed over to be crucified.

But here's what disturbs me.

**What if we never stopped making that choice?**

We punish honest employees because they threaten powerful interests.

We protect wrongdoing because the people responsible are useful to us.

We sacrifice competence for connections.

We call manipulation loyalty and cowardice diplomacy.

Then we wash our hands and pretend we had nothing to do with the outcome.

But this story isn't just about a crowd two thousand years ago.

It's about us.

**How many times have you supported what you knew was wrong simply because doing what was right would cost you something?**
