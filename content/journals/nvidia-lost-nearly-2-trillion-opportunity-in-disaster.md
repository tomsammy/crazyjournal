# NVIDIA LOST NEARLY $2 TRILLION. I SEE A POTENTIAL OPPORTUNITY WHERE OTHERS SEE A DISASTER.

*The stock has collapsed. But the business hasn't demonstrated a comparable collapse.*

**The Crazy Journal | November 2026**

> The headlines are frightening and Jensen Huang is gone. The stock has fallen approximately 35% from its October high. But Nvidia's latest quarterly revenue was $96.2 billion—up 106% year on year—with data centre alone generating $89 billion, and Amazon planning to deploy 2 million GPUs in 2027–2028. The stock has collapsed, but the business hasn't. Is fear temporarily making a powerful company more attractively priced?

![Nvidia Lost Nearly $2 Trillion: Opportunity in Disaster](/images/journals/nvidia-opportunity.jpg)

Jensen Huang is gone.

The headlines are frightening.

The stock has fallen approximately 35% from its October high.

But here's what interests me.

Nvidia's latest reported quarterly revenue was $96.2 billion.

Revenue grew 106% year on year.

Its data-centre business alone generated $89 billion.

And Amazon has announced plans to deploy another two million Nvidia GPUs in 2027–2028.

**The stock has collapsed. But the business hasn't demonstrated a comparable collapse.**

That's the disconnect I'm examining.

I might be wrong. The risks are real, and the stock could fall further.

But I believe this is precisely the kind of situation where investors should look beyond the headlines and examine the fundamentals.

**Is Nvidia genuinely worth less because its business has weakened—or has fear temporarily made a powerful company more attractively priced?**

That's my latest Crazy Journal.
